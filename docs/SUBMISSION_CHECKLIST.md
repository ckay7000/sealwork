# Sealwork: human submission checklist

Deadline: **November 14, 2026, 23:59 UTC**. Use the [official form](https://nearhackathon.com/#apply) or the API after final exact-payload confirmation. Re-read [the live guide](https://nearhackathon.com/HACKATHON.md) first. Submissions are immutable and each repository can be submitted once.

## Prepared project material

- Public repository, understandable README, use cases, development instructions and license.
- Live testnet application, explained overview and separate fresh uninterrupted paid/refund recordings, with captions and source evidence.
- Direct NEAR receipt checker with altered-record/code tests, plus a neutral bookkeeper test worksheet. Independent bookkeeper feedback remains uncollected.
- Independent-project and AI/library reuse disclosures.
- Proposed tracks: `privacy`, `agents`, `security`; these still need your final choice.

## Your remaining decisions and actions

1. Watch the demo, try the app and confirm what you want to claim in your own words.
2. Publish your own public X post saying the project was built at NEAR HACKATHON, with **@NEARProtocol #NEARHackathon** and the video or screenshots attached. [Draft prompts](X_DRAFTS.md) are provided for adaptation.
3. Supply and confirm project name, team lead name, contact email, team size, final track IDs, your mainnet NEAR prize-payout account, public repo URL and public X post URL. Do not create or share a private key.
4. Write your own 20–2000 character project description. Cover: whose problem this solves; the concrete working flow; what NEAR does; and the prototype’s important limits. The README and video are references for that explanation.
5. Read and decide on the exact consent notice: **“I agree that the organizers may use these details to review our submission and contact us about this hackathon.”**
6. Review every field of the final payload before approving submission. Keep that payload private. If using the API, generate one requestId and reuse it only for retries of that same submission.
7. Confirm a saved submission response. Keep the repository public and demo hosted through November 18, ideally November 25.

The official HACKATHON.md requires a human-written/published demo post and explicit confirmation of the exact submission payload. Personal information, consent and payout choices have not been invented. No application or social post has been sent.

Use your own wording to answer: Who struggles with this today? What action can they take in the live app? What visible result proves it works? Which exact NEAR contract or service enables that result? What is fictional, sponsored or still future work? Why is this independent of the other entry?
