Proposed tracks: `privacy`, `agents`, `security`. The human chooses final track IDs. The guide’s named integrations are suggestions, not mandatory requirements.

## Why those tracks fit


- `privacy`: encrypted financial input, private NEAR AI bank inference and OutLayer confidential computation.
- `agents`: constrained agent hiring and conditional payment for a completed task.
- `security`: pinned code, independent acceptance rules, fixed budgets and per-build outcomes constrain agent risk.

The finance example alone is not a claim of live stablecoin payroll or remittance rails. The demonstration pays a testnet processing fee, not the fictional bank amount.
